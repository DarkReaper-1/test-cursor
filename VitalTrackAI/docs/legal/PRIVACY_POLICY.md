## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use the VitalTrack AI iOS application and related optional services (the “Service”).

---

## 2. Summary (plain language)

- We built VitalTrack to be **privacy-first** and **offline-capable**.  
- Your health readings are stored in an **encrypted local database** on your device.  
- **iCloud / cloud sync** and **cloud AI insights** are **optional** and off until you opt in.  
- We **do not sell** your health data.  
- Blood pressure in the app comes from **external monitors** you use or import (manual, Bluetooth cuff, HealthKit, CSV)—not from the camera. Camera/Watch data is used for **heart rate**, not blood pressure.

---

## 3. Information we process

### 3.1 Health and wellness data (on device)

Depending on how you use the app, this may include:

- Blood pressure readings and metadata (source, device name, notes, tags)  
- Heart rate readings (camera PPG and/or HealthKit / Apple Watch)  
- Optional lifestyle logs (mood, water, sleep, exercise)  
- Medications and reminder schedules you enter  
- Personal goals/targets you set  
- Devices you pair (identifiers, names)  
- Insights generated from your data  

### 3.2 HealthKit

If you grant permission, we read and/or write health data types described in-app (e.g., blood pressure, heart rate, and optional sleep/activity/weight). You control HealthKit access in Apple’s Health app. We use HealthKit data to provide the Service (import/display/sync within the app), not to advertise to you.

### 3.3 Account and cloud data (optional)

If you enable optional cloud features, we may process:

- Sign in with Apple identifiers  
- Encrypted or protected sync payloads of the data you choose to sync  
- Aggregated statistics you submit for cloud AI insights (with consent)  
- Subscription entitlement status  

### 3.4 Purchase data

Purchases are processed by Apple. We receive entitlement information necessary to unlock Premium features. We do not store your full payment card number.

### 3.5 Diagnostics (non-health)

With your permission or as allowed by platform, we may collect coarse diagnostics (crash logs, performance, feature flags, error codes). We configure diagnostics to **avoid** blood pressure values, heart rate values, and free-text health notes.

### 3.6 Permissions metadata

Camera (heart rate PPG), Bluetooth (cuffs/scales), Notifications (reminders), Face ID/Touch ID (optional app lock), File access (CSV).

## 4. How we use information

- Provide tracking, charts, reminders, exports, and device connectivity  
- Generate **informational** insights (on-device heuristics; optional cloud AI)  
- Maintain security, prevent abuse, and debug issues  
- Honor Premium entitlements  
- Comply with law  

We do **not** use health data for third-party advertising or data broker sales.

## 5. Legal bases (GDPR / UK GDPR)

Where applicable, we rely on:

- **Contract** — to provide the Service you request  
- **Consent** — HealthKit access, notifications, cloud AI, optional analytics  
- **Legitimate interests** — security, fraud prevention, product improvement with minimized non-health diagnostics  
- **Legal obligation** — when required  

You may withdraw consent at any time in Settings or system settings without affecting prior lawful processing.

## 6. Sharing

We share information only:

- **With your direction** (e.g., Share Sheet exports you initiate)  
- **Service providers** under agreements (e.g., cloud hosting for optional features), prohibited from selling your health data  
- **Apple** for HealthKit, CloudKit/iCloud (if enabled), StoreKit, push notifications  
- **Legal requirements** or to protect rights and safety  

We do **not sell** personal information or health data as defined under CCPA/CPRA and similar laws, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

If optional cloud services process data outside your country, we use appropriate safeguards (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses) where required.

## 8. Retention

- **On device:** until you delete readings or use “Delete all data,” or uninstall (OS may retain until deletion).  
- **iCloud / our cloud (if enabled):** until you disable sync and delete cloud data, delete your account (if any), or request deletion—subject to legal retention.  
- **Backups:** Apple device/iCloud backups follow Apple’s policies when you include app data in backups.

## 9. Security

- Encrypted local database at rest  
- Key material stored in the iOS Keychain  
- TLS for network communication  
- Optional biometric app lock  
- Access controls for cloud systems  

No method is 100% secure; please use a strong device passcode.

## 13. Measurement honesty

We do not claim that the camera, flash, or fingerprint sensors measure blood pressure. Heart rate may be measured via PPG (camera/flash) or obtained from Apple Watch/HealthKit. Blood pressure is logged from external FDA-cleared monitors via manual entry, Bluetooth, HealthKit import, or CSV.
