**Headline:** Honest inputs. Clear trends.

**Body:** Your cuff measures blood pressure. VitalTrack stores it, charts it, and helps you prepare questions for your clinician. Your camera can help measure heart rate (PPG). Those are different jobs—and we keep them that way.

---

## 4. Feature blurbs

### Blood pressure logging
Capture readings from the monitors you already trust. Pair a compatible Bluetooth cuff, import from Apple Health, upload CSV, or enter numbers in seconds. Every entry shows its source.

### Heart rate (PPG)
Use the camera and flash to measure heart rate, or bring in Apple Watch heart rate via HealthKit. Labeled clearly as heart rate—never sold as blood pressure.

### Insights without the drama
On-device summaries highlight patterns in your log. Premium users can opt into cloud AI for richer language. No diagnoses. No dosing advice. Always: informational only—not medical advice.

### Privacy-first
Encrypted local database. Optional iCloud. HealthKit permissions you control. We do not sell health data.

### Free vs Premium
Core logging and basic charts stay free. Premium is advanced analytics, richer reports, and optional cloud insights—shown in a plain comparison table. Cancel anytime in Apple Subscriptions.

## 5. FAQ snippets (web)

**Can VitalTrack measure blood pressure with the camera?**  
No. The camera is for heart rate (PPG) only. Blood pressure comes from external FDA-cleared monitors via manual entry, Bluetooth, HealthKit, or CSV.

**Is this medical advice?**  
No. VitalTrack is informational only and not medical advice. Talk to a qualified clinician for decisions about your health.

**Do you sell my health data?**  
No.

**Do I need an account?**  
No for core tracking. Cloud sync and cloud AI are optional.
