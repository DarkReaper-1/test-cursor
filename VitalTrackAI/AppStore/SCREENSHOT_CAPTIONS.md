# App Store Screenshot Captions

Every screenshot caption must reinforce honesty. **Never** imply camera BP.

1. **Trust first** — “This app tracks blood pressure readings that you obtain from an FDA-cleared blood pressure monitor.”
2. **Dashboard** — “Today’s heart rate, latest cuff BP, and weekly trends — customizable cards.”
3. **Heart rate** — “Camera PPG and Apple Watch for BPM — not blood pressure.”
4. **Blood pressure** — “Manual, Bluetooth cuff, Apple Health, or CSV. No camera estimates.”
5. **AI insights** — “Informational patterns only. Not medical advice.”
6. **Devices** — “Pair compatible FDA-cleared Bluetooth monitors with clear recovery steps.”
7. **Reports** — “CSV, PDF, and doctor-ready summaries you control.”
8. **Subscription** — “Clear Free vs Premium. No hidden trials. Cancel anytime in Apple settings.”
