Before you start

VitalTrack is a wellness tool, not a medical device.

  • Heart rate is measured for real, using your device's camera and (when your browser allows it) its flash — a technique called photoplethysmography (PPG).
  • It does not measure blood pressure. Log readings from a real cuff instead.
  • Flash/torch control isn't available in every browser — notably iOS Safari has no web API for it at all. Without it, hold your fingertip gently over the lens near a bright lamp instead of in the dark.
  • Everything you enter stays in this browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to a server. Exporting a CSV is the only way data leaves.
  • Don't use this to diagnose or manage a medical condition — see a doctor if you feel unwell.

Not a medical device. No phone camera or fingerprint sensor measures blood pressure — VitalTrack estimates heart rate via real fingertip PPG and logs cuff-measured blood pressure you type in yourself. Runs entirely in your browser; nothing you enter is sent anywhere.
