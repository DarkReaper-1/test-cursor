# UX observations

## OBSERVED (public marketing + listings)

### Fantasy is the product

Arise sells a **System overlay on real training**. Store frames lead with awakening copy, Player identity, lightning HUD, and anime bodies—not with a calendar or a set logger.

### Four-tab IA

Work, quest, stats, and self are separate. “What should I do *right now*?” is split between Training cards and a Daily Quest sheet.

### Penalty as motivation

Screenshot 2 puts an orange warning on the quest itself: fail in time, take a penalty. Public reviews describe XP loss on missed days and say that is not motivating; they asked for a catch-up challenge instead.

### Streak + day count + level all visible

LVL, Day N, streak, rank, and class appear together. Identity is dense. Marketing even wallpapers “Leveled up!” behind the hero.

### Share-first social

First-party social is share cards and Instagram. Community is Discord, i.e. off-app.

### Nutrition is a plan, not a coach conversation (in the stills)

A kcal/macro target and a weight curve. No chat, no pantry input, no confirmation of AI interpretation on these frames.

### Accessibility not declared

App Store accessibility section is empty. Touch targets in marketing look large; contrast is high; color is also used as rank meaning (gold/teal/red outlines).

### Paywall is part of the advertised loop

Listing: subscription required for plans, quests, and features. Play reviews: cannot evaluate gamification without paying.

### Reliability is a trust issue

Public reports: gray screen, login errors, random sign-out, history wipe after rest days, rest timer dying in background, flaky support site.

### Aesthetic lock-in

Cyan-on-navy HUD + manhwa hero + E–SSS ranks + “Player” is tightly coupled to Solo Leveling-class imagery. That is a category magnet and a legal/brand trap for anyone who copies it.

---

## INFERRED

- The daily 100/100/100 quest and the “smart” generated split may compete for attention.
- Fatigue (screenshot 4) may be cosmetic or a real governor; behavior unconfirmed.
- Photo logging is proof-of-presence, not pose analysis.
- “AI” in store copy may mean template generation plus rules, not a tool-using coach with memory.
- Rank share exists because the fantasy is social status, not because the training science needs letter grades.

---

## PROPOSED Helix UX principles

1. **One question on launch:** What should I do now, and why?
2. **Cinematic but quiet.** Depth and type, not constant lightning.
3. **Recovery over humiliation.** Momentum drops; a Recovery Directive appears.
4. **Confirm before commit.** NL and vision are proposals.
5. **Character ≠ medical record.** Biometrics are behind privacy controls.
6. **Feel the loop before pay.**
7. **Color never sole status.** Rank, complete, and warning use shape + label.
8. **Empty, loading, and error are designed states.** Never a gray root.
9. **Coach is contextual** on Today and after sessions, not a fifth junk tab on day one.
10. **Original language:** Operator, Directive, Lattice, Calibration—not Player, Awakening, Hunter, Shadow.
