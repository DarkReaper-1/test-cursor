## Description

VitalTrack helps you track your heart rate and blood pressure over time,
understand your trends, and share meaningful reports with your doctor —
without the misleading claims or clutter common in this category.

**Be clear about this first:** VitalTrack does not and cannot measure
blood pressure using your iPhone's camera, fingerprint sensor, or any
built-in sensor. No app can. VitalTrack tracks blood pressure readings
you obtain from your own FDA-cleared blood pressure monitor — entered by
hand, synced automatically from a Bluetooth-enabled cuff, imported from
Apple Health, or imported from a CSV file.

**Heart rate**, on the other hand, VitalTrack measures directly: cover
your camera and flash with your fingertip for about 20 seconds
(photoplethysmography — the same principle behind a pulse oximeter), and
get your BPM with a live signal-quality indicator so you know when to
trust the reading.

**What's included:**
- Camera-based heart rate measurement with live signal-quality feedback
- Manual blood pressure logging in seconds
- Bluetooth sync with standard BLE blood pressure monitors
- Apple Health and CSV import
- Interactive trend charts — 7 days to a year
- Weekly, monthly, and yearly averages
- Smart Insights: on-device pattern detection (not a diagnosis — see FAQ)
- Local reminders for readings, medication, hydration, exercise, and
  appointments
- CSV and PDF export, including a doctor-ready summary report
- Dark mode, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver support
- No account required. No ads.

**Free plan:** unlimited manual logging, heart rate measurement, 30-day
history, CSV export.

**VitalTrack Premium** ($4.99/month or $39.99/year): unlimited history,
PDF doctor reports, Bluetooth device sync, iCloud sync across your
devices. No hidden trial — you see the price before you're ever asked to
pay, and you can cancel anytime from your Apple ID subscription settings.

VitalTrack is a wellness tool, not a medical device, and does not provide
medical advice or diagnosis. If you have concerns about your health, talk
to a doctor.
