# VitalTrack Terms of Service (draft)

_Last updated: [date of actual publication]_

## 1. What VitalTrack is

VitalTrack is a wellness application that helps you record and visualize
heart rate and blood pressure readings. It is **not a medical device**,
does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and is not a
substitute for professional medical care.

## 2. What VitalTrack cannot do

VitalTrack cannot measure blood pressure using your device's camera,
fingerprint sensor, or any other built-in sensor. Blood pressure figures
shown in the app always originate from a source you control: your own
manual entry, a Bluetooth-connected blood pressure monitor, an Apple
Health import, or a CSV import. VitalTrack does not verify the accuracy
of readings from third-party devices or files you import.

Heart rate measured by VitalTrack's camera feature is an estimate based
on photoplethysmography and may be affected by motion, finger placement,
skin tone, and lighting. It is not equivalent to a clinical-grade device.

## 3. No medical advice

Nothing in VitalTrack, including Smart Insights, constitutes medical
advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations. Insights are
automatically generated, rule-based observations about patterns in your
own logged data and may be wrong, incomplete, or not relevant to your
health situation. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider with
questions about a medical condition, and seek emergency care for urgent
symptoms rather than relying on this app.

## 4. Your account and data

VitalTrack does not require an account to use its core features. Data
you enter is stored on your device and, only if you enable it, in your
personal iCloud account or Apple Health. See the Privacy Policy for
details.

## 5. Subscriptions

VitalTrack Premium is an auto-renewing subscription billed through your
Apple ID, at the price shown in the app before purchase. You can manage
or cancel your subscription at any time in your iPhone's Settings app
under your name > Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewal; access
continues until the end of the period already paid for. We do not offer
hidden trial periods that convert to paid subscriptions without a price
being shown to you first.

## 6. Acceptable use

You agree not to use VitalTrack to store or transmit anyone else's health
information without their consent, and not to attempt to reverse-engineer,
disrupt, or misuse the app's Bluetooth or HealthKit integrations.

## 7. Disclaimer of warranties

VitalTrack is provided "as is." We do not warrant that measurements,
imports, or insights will be error-free, uninterrupted, or fit for any
particular purpose, medical or otherwise.

## 8. Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, VitalTrack's developer is not
liable for any decision made in reliance on data or insights shown in the
app, including decisions about medical care. This is not a limitation on
liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence where such a
limitation would be unlawful.

## 9. Changes

We may update these terms; material changes will be surfaced in the app.
