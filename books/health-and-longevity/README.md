# Health & Longevity

**A three-part book on living longer, preventing chronic disease, and sustaining energy for life.**

| Part | Title | Focus |
|------|-------|-------|
| I | [The Longevity Blueprint](01-the-longevity-blueprint.md) | How to live longer, avoid chronic disease, and stay energetic into old age |
| II | [Reverse Your Health](02-reverse-your-health.md) | A practical system for preventing obesity, diabetes, high blood pressure, and heart disease |
| III | [The Energy Code](03-the-energy-code.md) | Sleep, nutrition, exercise, and stress management to maximize physical and mental energy |

## How This Book Is Organized

Each part stands alone as a complete guide, but they build on one another:

1. **The Longevity Blueprint** lays the foundation — the biology of aging and the handful of habits with the strongest evidence behind them.
2. **Reverse Your Health** turns that foundation into a step-by-step protocol for the four conditions responsible for most preventable death and disability.
3. **The Energy Code** closes the loop by tuning the daily systems — sleep, food, movement, and stress — that determine how you feel today while you build a longer tomorrow.

Read the parts in order for a full curriculum, or jump straight to the one that matches your current goal.
